Hi, I'm Jacob.

I'm a physics Ph.D. student at Northeastern University, where I work with Ning Bao on quantum information and quantum gravity. Mostly I think about holography: how the geometry of a spacetime is written into the entanglement of the quantum system living on its boundary.

I also like building things. I use machine learning on problems in quantum error correction and many-body physics, and on the side I built Supreme Physics, an online physics tutoring platform that I still maintain.

Research

In AdS/CFT, a theory of gravity is equivalent to a conformal field theory one dimension down. I'm interested in what quantum information theory can tell us about that dictionary, and lately that has meant:

  • reflected entropy and coarse-grained tensor networks in 3D gravity;
  • phase transitions and uberholography in pure-state geometries;
  • the combinatorics of holographic quantum secret sharing;
  • the capacity of entanglement and holographic entropies at finite resources.

On the computational side, I'm training graph neural network decoders for the triangular color code (built on its LightsOut formulation), working on decoders for non-abelian topological codes, and using normalizing flows to learn the spectrum of the SYK model at sizes exact diagonalization can't reach.

Before grad school I worked on quantum fields around rotating black holes at Grinnell College, and on jet substructure with the CMS heavy-ion group at École Polytechnique.
