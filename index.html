Research

I'm broadly interested in two areas, and most of all in what each can teach us about the other.

Quantum information

Quantum error correction, von Neumann algebras, and entanglement in quantum field theory.

Quantum gravity

Holography and AdS/CFT, black hole physics, and the gravitational path integral.

Computationally, I write and maintain research code in Python, PyTorch, C++ and Mathematica and run it at scale on HPC clusters. I've built graph neural network decoders for topological codes, normalizing-flow models of SYK spectral statistics, and finite element solvers for semiclassical black hole physics. I'm always glad to talk with people thinking about machine learning for theoretical physics.
